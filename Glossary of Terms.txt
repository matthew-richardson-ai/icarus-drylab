Glossary (abbreviations & symbols)

AI – Artificial Intelligence.

ML – Machine Learning.

IP – Intellectual Property.

LoD – Limit of Detection: the lowest concentration of analyte (target molecule) you can detect with a specified success rate.

SNR – Signal‑to‑Noise Ratio.

ADC – Analog‑to‑Digital Converter: electronics that turn analog signals into digital numbers.

AR(1) – Autoregressive (order 1) process: a simple time‑series model where the present value depends on the previous value plus noise.

FFT – Fast Fourier Transform: converts time‑domain signals to frequency domain.

CNN – Convolutional Neural Network.

TCN – Temporal Convolutional Network.

ROC – Receiver Operating Characteristic curve (true positive rate vs false positive rate).

PR curve – Precision‑Recall curve.

CI – Confidence Interval.

DOE – Design of Experiments: a plan for systematically varying conditions to probe performance.

k_on, k_off – Binding kinetics constants; association (on‑rate) and dissociation (off‑rate) measured in appropriate units (e.g., M⁻¹·s⁻¹ and s⁻¹).

θ (theta) – Fraction of sensor binding sites that are occupied (dimensionless, 0–1).

C – Analyte concentration (e.g., colony‑forming units per milliliter, CFU/mL, or molarity).

S – Sensor signal (arbitrary units mapped by the sensor/electronics).

TFLite – TensorFlow Lite: a format for running neural networks on embedded devices.

ONNX – Open Neural Network Exchange: a common format for ML models.
